I am a Member of Technical Staff at [Reka AI](https://reka.ai/), working on large-scale multimodal and video foundation models. I am a core contributor to pre-training [Rho-1](https://reka.ai/labs/research/rho-1-collapsing-the-multimodal-stack), a 19B omni world model that understands and generates text, images, and video and outputs robot actions in a single network, and [Marey](https://www.moonvalley.com/marey), Moonvalley's text-to-video model trained on fully licensed data. I lead reference control and structural control for both models. Before that, I was a Senior Research Scientist at ByteDance (2020 - 2024), where I worked on the company's first-generation text-to-image foundation model and on AIGC effects for TikTok and Douyin. I received my Ph.D. from [Rutgers University](http://www.rutgers.edu/), supervised by [Professor Ahmed Elgammal](https://www.cs.rutgers.edu/~elgammal/Home.html). My research interests include **video generation**, **world models**, **controllable and reference-based generation**, and **multimodal foundation models**. Email: zyzflying at gmail dot com. For more information on my education and work experience, see my [CV](https://yzzhu0.github.io/cv/).
